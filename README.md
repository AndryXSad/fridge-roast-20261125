One line of plain text, at most 200 characters, roasting a smart fridge: its
app, its firmware updates, its camera, its ice maker, its opinions about
your milk. A judge model reads the lines two at a time and keeps the
funnier one.

> My fridge ran a self-diagnostic and concluded the problem is me. It then suggested I upgrade to a newer model of human.

The line was chosen by a local pairwise tournament over 16 fresh candidates,
scored on the judge brief's five criteria in order: a roast of the smart
fridge itself; a punchline that lands on first read; specific to smart
fridges; tight wording; a fresh angle that dodges the obvious cracks about
the fridge ordering groceries or watching you. It won its group 3-0, both
semifinal and final pairings. See `CANDIDATES.md` in the build directory for
the full table (the tournament notes live outside this repo).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`).
